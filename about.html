About us

Why Nova Office exists

A small, founder-led company building digital tools for small businesses — one working product at a time.

Our mission

To build practical, affordable digital tools and websites for small businesses. We are a young company, and we develop our offering one working product at a time.

Our approach

We release a working version first and improve it with use. We announce a product only when it is available, and we state clearly which products are still in development.

Founder

Mohnish Kumar

MK

Nova Office is founded and run by Mohnish Kumar (MK), who builds each product directly rather than outsourcing development. The company is deliberately small, so every tool it ships reflects an actual, tested use case rather than a sales pitch.

What we stand for

Four commitments

Practical

Every tool solves a real, everyday business task.

Affordable

Simple technology, kept lean, so cost stays within reach of small teams.

Transparent

We state clearly what is available and what is still in development.

Clear

Plain language and straightforward interfaces, without unnecessary jargon.
