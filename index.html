About

Useful software, without the big-company price tag

Our mission

To build practical, affordable digital tools and websites for small businesses. We are a young company, and we are growing it one real, working product at a time.

Our approach

We start small, ship something that works, and improve it. We would rather launch one useful tool than announce ten that do not exist yet.

  • Practical

    Every tool solves a real, everyday business task.

  • Affordable

    Simple technology, kept lean, so cost stays within reach of small teams.

  • Honest

    We say clearly what is ready and what is still being built.

  • Clear

    Plain language and simple interfaces, with no jargon.
