About

About RENOVA OFFICE

Our mission

To build practical, affordable digital tools and websites for small businesses. We are a young company and we develop our offering one working product at a time.

Our approach

We release a working version first and improve it with use. We announce a product only when it is available, and we state clearly which products are still in development.

  • Practical

    Every tool solves a real, everyday business task.

  • Affordable

    Simple technology, kept lean, so cost stays within reach of small teams.

  • Transparent

    We state clearly what is available and what is still in development.

  • Clear

    Plain language and straightforward interfaces, without unnecessary jargon.

Services

Our services

Our work is organised into three areas, which we are developing progressively.

Business tools & document generators

Simple web tools for everyday paperwork, such as documents and routine business forms, that you can use straight from your browser.

Website development for small businesses

Clean, fast, mobile-friendly websites that present your business clearly and are easy to keep up to date.

Productivity tools

Lightweight tools that help you organise your day and manage tasks, without the clutter of heavy software.
